InstaVault for UGC{Gen}

Settings, privacy & help
InstaVault is an unofficial tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.

Download settings

A modest delay keeps large batches orderly. Downloads run sequentially.

Available tokens: {username}, {type}, {date}, {id}, {sequence}.

UGC{Gen}

Where your UGC{Gen} server runs. Selected media is sent there as source clips for Research & adapt. No key is needed: the server accepts captures only from a browser extension.

Privacy by design

  • No analytics, advertising, telemetry, or remote code.
  • No passwords, cookies, session tokens, or private browser storage are read.
  • Collected links and settings stay on this device unless you explicitly send selected media to your UGC{Gen} server.
  • Page access is limited to Instagram, its media delivery hosts, and your configured UGC{Gen} address.
  • Media is downloaded directly by Chrome using links already present in the page you can view.

Responsible use

Download only content you created, own, or have explicit permission to save. You are responsible for complying with copyright, privacy rights, local law, and Instagram’s Terms of Use. Do not use this extension to evade access controls or redistribute other people’s content.

How it works

  1. Sign in to Instagram normally and open a post, reel, story, highlight, profile, Explore, tagged, or saved page you are allowed to view.
  2. Use the popup to collect what is visible, or open the library and scan as you scroll.
  3. Filter, manually select media, choose a sequence, and download.

Instagram changes its site often. If an item is missing, open it directly and collect again. Expired CDN links can be refreshed by reloading the Instagram page. The extension intentionally does not call private Instagram APIs or bypass availability restrictions.
